Real CUDA mapping



In a real CUDA kernel, each logical thread computes its global index. Extra launched threads are skipped by the `if (i < n)` guard. Execution order and SM placement are not simulated.




What did you just learn?
The restaurant team is a thread block; each cook is a CUDA thread. The GPU runs many blocks, but the hardware decides when and on which SM to schedule them. A block is not a warp: NVIDIA warps group 32 threads for execution.
